Wage & Payment Rules

Fair pay, no hidden fees

percent
0% commission for workers Workers keep 100% of the agreed wage. Turontoo never takes a cut from worker pay.
lock
Escrow protection Wages are reserved in escrow before the job starts. Workers are guaranteed payment on completion.
schedule
Payment within 24 hours Cash on finish or bKash — the choice is set at posting and cannot change after booking.
warning
Never pay off-platform Requests to pay via personal bKash, cash outside escrow, or advance fees are a red flag. Report them.

Suspension Policy

How accounts get restricted

Turontoo reviews every report. Accounts can be restricted, warned, or suspended depending on the severity and frequency of violations.

1
First warning Minor issues (late arrival, unclear description) — warning only.
2
3-5 reports If 3-5 different users report the same account with valid evidence, the account is flagged for review.
3
Suspension Serious violations (fraud, harassment, illegal activity) → immediate suspension. Wallet funds are held for 30 days pending investigation.
undo
Appeal You can appeal within 14 days by contacting support. Our team reviews every appeal.
